Frequently asked questions

Helpful details about package pricing, our money-back guarantee, report access and payment requirements.

What does the BEST CARFAX cost?+

AutoVerify currently offers three report package options: Silver — $47.99, Gold — $68.99 and Platinum — $89.99.

What does the money-back guarantee mean?+

If you are not satisfied with your report, you may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refund eligibility and the steps to request a refund will be governed by AutoVerify's Terms and Conditions.

How long can I access my purchased vehicle history report?+

You can access your purchased report for 30 days after purchase through your account or the link in your order confirmation. Updates for the vehicle you checked are included during this 30-day access period. Access expires after 30 days.

What do I need to purchase a vehicle history report?+

You need a valid email address and a supported credit or debit card to purchase a report. Available payment methods will be confirmed when secure checkout is activated.
