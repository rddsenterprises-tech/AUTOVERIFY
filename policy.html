Transparency · Customer information

Policies & service information

Understand how vehicle information, customer details, report requests and service enquiries are intended to be handled by AutoVerify.

01 · Data handling

Privacy policy

Information that may be collected

When customer services become active, AutoVerify may request information needed to respond to an enquiry or fulfil a report request, such as a name, email address, order reference, vehicle identification number (VIN), selected package and transaction status. Customers should not submit passwords, full payment-card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information through general contact channels.

How information may be used

  • To respond to service and support enquiries.
  • To identify a requested vehicle and selected report package.
  • To coordinate order processing, payment verification and report delivery when those services are enabled.
  • To investigate suspected misuse, protect the service and maintain appropriate business records.

VIN and vehicle information

A VIN identifies a vehicle and may be shared with a vehicle-data provider only if and when a provider integration is activated and the customer has been clearly informed. The website currently validates the VIN field in the browser and temporarily stores the entered value in the current browsing session to carry it to the pricing page. It does not retrieve official vehicle records or send the VIN to a provider.

Sharing and service providers

If services launch, limited information may need to be shared with contracted providers such as vehicle-data suppliers, hosting, email, payment and technical-support services. The final policy must identify the actual providers, their roles and relevant data handling terms. AutoVerify should not sell personal information. Any provider or data-transfer arrangement will be identified here when an active service relationship is established.

Retention, access and security

Information should be retained only for documented business, support, legal and security purposes, then securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate. The final policy must specify retention periods and a verified process for privacy requests. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Cookies, analytics and external links

The website may use essential browser storage to support navigation between pages. The current VIN flow uses session storage. If analytics, advertising cookies or additional tracking are introduced, the relevant notice and consent controls should be added. External websites have their own privacy practices.

02 · Service conditions

Terms of service

Purpose of vehicle reports

Vehicle history information is intended to help customers conduct research before a vehicle purchase. Reports may include records such as title status, reported accidents or damage, odometer entries, recalls, theft indicators, ownership, registration, service, lien or auction information, depending on the selected package and provider coverage.

Data availability and accuracy

Records come from external sources and may be incomplete, delayed, unavailable or inaccurate. A missing record does not prove that an event never occurred. AutoVerify does not claim that every vehicle will have a complete history or that every listed data category will be available for every VIN.

Not an inspection or purchase recommendation

A vehicle history report is not a mechanical inspection, safety certification, title guarantee, legal opinion or substitute for checking original documents. Customers should arrange an independent inspection and verify the vehicle, seller, ownership documents and any material concerns before purchase.

Customer responsibilities

  • Enter the VIN carefully and check it against the vehicle.
  • Provide a valid email address for any future report delivery.
  • Use reports only for lawful and authorised purposes.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, misuse or gain unauthorised access to the website or its systems.

Packages and changes

Package names, prices and listed features describe the available service options. Actual report availability depends on provider coverage for the vehicle. Any applicable taxes, currency, delivery estimates and service limitations will be displayed before an order can be submitted.

03 · Orders and payment

Refund & cancellation policy

Order and payment availability

Online checkout is currently unavailable. Customers cannot submit paid orders or make payments through the website at this time. Selecting a package does not create an order or confirm a purchase.

Before a future purchase

Before checkout is activated, AutoVerify must publish a final refund policy explaining when a customer can cancel, how to report an issue, the deadline for submitting a request, what evidence may be needed, how refunds are assessed and the expected processing time. The policy must also explain what happens if a provider cannot return a report or the VIN is entered incorrectly.

Refund requests

Once a verified support channel and order process are active, customers should provide their order reference and purchase email when asking for help. Never send full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or banking credentials in a support message.

Payment security

Any future payment should be handled through a confirmed payment processor and its secure checkout flow. AutoVerify should not request or store a customer's full card details directly. Accepted methods, currency, taxes and payment confirmation steps must be displayed before the customer submits an order.

04 · Fulfilment

Report preparation & delivery

Intended delivery process

The proposed service flow is: customer submits a VIN, reviews a package, provides contact details, completes an enabled payment process, and receives a report after the order and provider response have been verified. The website is not currently connected to an official vehicle-data provider and does not automate report delivery.

Delivery channel and timing

Email delivery is the intended method shown in the website content. Delivery timelines, support hours, retry procedures and handling of bounced or incorrect email addresses will be communicated when report fulfilment is available. No fixed delivery time is guaranteed.

Report limitations

Report availability depends on the provider and records accessible for the vehicle. If a report cannot be obtained, the customer should be informed through the verified support process and the final refund or alternative-resolution policy should apply.

05 · Responsible use

Acceptable use & intellectual property

Permitted use

Customers may use the website to learn about the service, submit genuine enquiries and, once enabled, request vehicle reports for lawful purposes. Do not submit false information, impersonate another person, interfere with the service, scrape or overload the website, or use information in a way that violates applicable law or third-party rights.

Website materials

Unless otherwise stated, website text, branding, layout and original materials are intended for AutoVerify business use and may not be copied, republished or commercially reused without permission. Third-party names, marks and data remain the property of their respective owners. Any third-party report provider relationship must be accurately disclosed and must not be implied without authorisation.

06 · Updates and contact

Policy updates & questions

Changes to these policies

These notices may be revised as the service, provider integrations and legal requirements are confirmed. The final published policy should show its effective date and explain how material changes will be communicated to customers.

Questions and requests

For general enquiries, visit the Contact page. A verified support email and any formal privacy-request contact details must be configured before launch. Do not send confidential payment information through general enquiries.

Applicable law

The governing law, dispute-resolution process and any mandatory consumer rights depend on the business's confirmed operating location and the markets it serves. These details are not specified here and should be confirmed with the business before relying on them.
