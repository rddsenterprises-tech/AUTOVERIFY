Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you begin your vehicle search.

Where can I find the VIN? +

A VIN is commonly visible near the lower corner of the windshield on the driver's side and on vehicle registration or insurance documents. Always compare the number with the vehicle itself.

Does every report contain the same information? +

No. Available details can vary by vehicle, package and data-provider records. Some vehicles may have limited or no records for particular events.

Can a vehicle history report replace an inspection? +

No. A history report is one source of information. Consider an independent mechanical inspection and verify important documents before buying.

Are online orders and reports available right now? +

Online checkout is currently unavailable, and this website does not retrieve official vehicle records. Report availability depends on vehicle-data coverage and service activation.
